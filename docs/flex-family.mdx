## Bring the family together

**FLEX Fields + Media Studio.** Keep the FLEX upload experience while choosing existing Studio files or uploading new ones into an approved workspace. Add the Studio picker to FlexRichEditor to reuse images in your content. [Explore the integration](/flex-fields)

**FLEX Theme + Filament.** Give the host panel its own colors, typography, branding and layout. Configure native forms, actions and navigation within the panel's theme. [Explore FLEX Theme](https://flex-theme.bjanczak.com/)

**Media Studio across your application.** Use the same media library from form fields, editors, tables and record details. Keep references to shared files and let Studio manage authorized delivery. [Explore Filament components](/filament)

Each product has its own installation and configuration. Choose the components your application needs, then connect them through the documented integrations.
